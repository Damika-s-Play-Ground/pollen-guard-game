Tower combinations · 6 pairings

Golden Seeds · Sunflower + Sprout

Activation: The Sprout is closer than the Sunflower’s current radius: 122 base, 190 with Golden Pollen at level 2, or 225 at level 3.

Bonus: The Sprout’s attack interval is 12% shorter.

Limit: One Sunflower source applies per Sprout. Duplicate Sunflowers do not stack; unrelated pairs receive no haste.

Frosted Seeds · Dew Bell + Sprout Slinger

Activation: Keep the Sprout closer than 108 map units to a Dew Bell.

Bonus: Sprout shots slow ground pests for 0.30s. This can combine with Golden Seeds’ separate rate bonus.

Limit: One Dew source applies per Sprout; extra Dew Bells do not increase the slow.

Steam Burst · Dew Bell + Ember Moth

Activation: Keep the Ember Moth closer than 108 map units to a Dew Bell.

Bonus: Ember shots splash ground pests within 32 map units of impact and slow them for 0.45s.

Limit: One Dew source applies per Ember Moth. Extra Dew Bells do not increase the splash or slow; flying pests are not hit.

Sporecap · Briar Patch + Puff Pod

Activation: Keep the Puff Pod closer than 100 map units to a Briar Patch.

Bonus: Puff blasts poison hit ground pests for 1.0s.

Limit: One Briar source applies per Puff Pod. Extra Briars do not extend the poison; flying pests are not hit.

Windcarved Thorns · Kite Keeper + Thorn Beacon

Activation: Keep the Thorn Beacon closer than 132 map units to a Kite Keeper.

Bonus: Thorn hits push eligible ground and air pests back by 7 map units.

Limit: One Kite Keeper source applies per Thorn Beacon. Extra Kite Keepers do not increase the push.

Cyclone Cap · Kite Keeper + Puff Pod

Activation: Keep the Puff Pod closer than 120 map units to a Kite Keeper.

Bonus: Puff blasts push hit ground pests back by 10 map units. It can combine with Sporecap’s poison in the same blast.

Limit: One Kite Keeper applies per Puff Pod; extra Kites do not increase the push. Flyers remain outside the Puff Pod’s normal targeting.

Terrain & placement bonuses
  • Fertile: attack interval 8% shorter; Sunflower earns 3 extra petals per cleared wave.
  • Water edge: normal Dew Bell attacks slow for 15% longer; normal Briar attacks slow and poison for 15% longer.
  • High ground: attacking defenders gain 12% range.
  • Ordinary grass: baseline stats; all defenders remain fully usable.

Research pathways · sidegrades

Chapter clears and every three mastery medals grant research points. Unlock nodes, then equip at most one choice from each path. Refit for free between missions; challenge variants use standard, research-free rules.

Relic loadout · two slots

How to defend the meadow

1. Place defenders beside the trail.
2. Start waves and earn petals from each mite.
3. Upgrade towers or change their target priority.
4. Sunflowers add petals after each clear; Bloom pulse charges as you pop.

Mission kit: the Meadow Guardian moves over safe grass and can slow nearby ground pests with Pollen Pulse (18-second cooldown). Ground pests can knock it down for 4 seconds. Pollen Ward adds 2 starting seeds in every ruleset; Sudden Death with Ward is clearly labeled as a 3-seed variant. You can also play without a kit. The Guardian is optional; every core mission can be played without it.
